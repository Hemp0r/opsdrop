# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-slug.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the issue we are facing? What forces are at play (technical, product,
operational)? State the problem and constraints, not the solution. Anyone reading
this later should understand the situation without already knowing the answer.

## Decision

The change we are making, stated in active voice: "We will …". Be specific.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include positive, negative, and
neutral outcomes, follow-up work this creates, and anything a future reader might
mistake for an oversight.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were rejected and the reason each was not chosen. This is the
part that saves a future reader from re-litigating the decision.
